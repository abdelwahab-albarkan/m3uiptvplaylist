## IPTV Smarters Pro: Where to get it

### Essential recommendations for IPTV Smarters Pro

Always install from an official store or the developer's official website:

- **Android phones and tablets:** Google Play.
- **iPhone, iPad and Apple TV:** the App Store.
- **Fire TV:** check the Amazon Appstore first; otherwise use Downloader with the developer's official download address, as described in the [Fire TV guide](/setup/firestick).
- **Windows and macOS:** the developer's site offers desktop versions.
- **Samsung and LG:** versions have been offered on some TV models; search your TV's store.

Avoid "Smarters Pro APK" files from download aggregator sites. Repackaged copies can contain adware or worse, and some ship with a stranger's playlist preloaded.

## Log in with Xtream Codes (recommended)

1. Open the app and accept the terms.
2. Choose **Login with Xtream Codes API**.
3. Fill in the fields:
   - **Any name:** a label for yourself, such as *Living Room*
   - **Username** and **Password:** exactly as given, since they're case-sensitive
   - **URL:** the full server address, for example `http://server.example:8080`
4. Tap **Add user**. Smarters downloads channels, movies, series and the guide.

## Log in with an M3U link

Choose **Load your playlist or file/URL**, select **M3U URL**, and paste the link. M3U logins work, but the movies and series sections are usually less organized than with Xtream.

## Features worth knowing

- **Live TV with a mini guide:** press OK while watching to see what's on now and next.
- **Catch-up:** if your provider supports it, channels with a clock icon let you replay recent programs.
- **Multi-screen:** watch up to four channels at once on capable devices. Each one counts against your connection limit.
- **Parental controls:** set a PIN for categories under **Settings**, then **Parental Control**.
- **External player:** under **Settings**, then **Player Selection**, you can route playback through VLC or MX Player if the built-in player struggles with a format.

## Fixing common Smarters errors

| Message | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invalid username or password | Typo, expired plan, or wrong server format | Paste each field again; confirm the plan is active |
| No internet connection / Server not responding | Server address wrong, or blocked by your network | Check the URL and port; try mobile data to rule out your router |
| Channels list but won't play | Codec or connection limit | Try another channel; close other devices; switch the player in settings |
| EPG not showing | Guide not downloaded | **Settings**, then **EPG**, then **Refresh EPG** |

For deeper fixes, see [IPTV not working](/troubleshooting/iptv-not-working).

## Smarters vs TiviMate

Smarters is simpler and available on far more devices. [TiviMate](/apps/tivimate) has a better live TV guide and faster channel switching, but it only runs on Android TV devices. Many people use Smarters on phones and TiviMate on the living room TV.


When choosing a reliable **IPTV Smarters Pro**, verifying network peering and server routing ensures optimal playback stability.
