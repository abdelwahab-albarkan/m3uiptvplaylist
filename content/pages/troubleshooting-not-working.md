## IPTV Not Working: Quick check: is it the login or the network?

### Essential recommendations for IPTV Not Working

1. **Try the same login on a phone using mobile data**, with Wi-Fi off. Use any player that accepts Xtream Codes or M3U.
   - **Works on mobile data:** your login is fine. The problem is your home network or the TV device.
   - **Fails on mobile data too:** the problem is the login or the server. Check the error table below, then contact support.
2. **Try a different player app** on the same device. If the second app works, the first app is misconfigured or outdated.

## Error messages decoded

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Authorization failed* / *Invalid login* / HTTP **401** | Username, password or server isn't accepted | Paste each field again; check for spaces; confirm your plan is active |
| *Max connections reached* / *Too many devices* | More devices are streaming than your plan allows | Close the player on other devices; wait two minutes; try again |
| HTTP **403 Forbidden** | The server refused your connection, sometimes because of your IP address or network | Restart the router; test on mobile data; contact support |
| HTTP **404 Not Found** / *Playlist not found* | The server address or path is wrong, or the playlist link changed | Re-copy the server URL from your latest welcome email |
| HTTP **5xx** / *Server not responding* | The server is overloaded or down | Wait 10–15 minutes; if it persists, contact support |
| *Unable to parse playlist* | The M3U link returned something that isn't a playlist | Open the link in a browser; if it shows a web page or error, the link is wrong |

## Black screen, no audio, or frozen picture

- **Black screen with sound:** a video codec problem. Switch the player's decoder between hardware and software, or try a different player engine. Our [XCIPTV guide](/apps/xciptv) explains engine switching.
- **Picture but no sound:** turn off audio passthrough in the player or device. Your TV or soundbar may not support the stream's audio format, such as AC3 or E-AC3.
- **Frozen on the first frame:** usually a buffering problem. Go to the [buffering guide](/troubleshooting/iptv-buffering).

## The guide is empty or wrong

If channels play but the TV guide shows *No information*, the EPG data hasn't loaded or doesn't match your channels. Refresh the EPG in the player settings, then see our [EPG guide](/guides/iptv-epg) for matching and time-shift fixes.

## Movies and series are missing

With an M3U login, many players don't show movies and series in their own sections. Switching to the **Xtream Codes** login usually fixes this. Read more in [M3U playlists vs Xtream Codes](/guides/m3u-playlist).

## Download the player from a real source

People often search for "IPTV Smarters Pro uptodown" or other mirror sites when an app disappears from their store. Mirror sites can host outdated or modified versions, and a modified player is a common reason for crashes and strange errors. Reinstall from the official store or the developer's own website instead.
