## Our global streaming infrastructure

Delivering live broadcast video over the open internet requires robust networking engineering. Video streams are not static web pages; they represent continuous, high-bitrate data feeds that cannot tolerate packet loss or latency spikes.

Our technical architecture is built on three core pillars:

1. **High-Capacity CDN Edge Routing:** Rather than routing all viewers to a single centralized server, our system distributes traffic across multiple high-bandwidth edge nodes. Viewers automatically connect to the physically nearest node, minimizing transit hops and eliminating peak-hour network bottlenecks.
2. **True 50/60 FPS Broadcast Cadence:** High-motion live sports look choppy when compressed to standard 30 FPS. Our broadcast headends ingest official source feeds and preserve full 50 and 60 frames per second motion clarity.
3. **Automated Redundant Failover:** Every major sports and premium entertainment channel is connected to primary, secondary, and tertiary backup feeds. If an upstream broadcast glitch occurs, our load balancers switch to an alternative source within milliseconds.
