## IPTV Refund Policy: Our policy

### Essential recommendations for IPTV Refund Policy

The current refund terms are shown above. They apply to every plan purchased directly from IPTV M3U Playlist.

## Before you request a refund

Most problems in the first few days are setup issues that can be fixed quickly: a typo in the server address, the wrong player for your device, or a weak Wi-Fi signal at the TV. Before requesting a refund, try:

- The [setup guide for your device](/setup)
- The [troubleshooting triage](/troubleshooting)
- Contacting [support](/contact) with your device, player app and the exact error

## How to request a refund

Contact us through the [contact page](/contact) with the topic *Something else*, and include:

1. The email address used for your order
2. Your order date and plan
3. A short description of the problem and what you've already tried

We confirm every request by email and explain the next step. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## What we look at

When reviewing a request, we look at whether the service could be set up on your devices, whether the problem was on our side or on your local network, and how long the subscription has been in use. We'll explain our decision in plain language either way.

## Payment disputes

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, please contact us first. Most billing questions are resolved quickly once we can see the order details, and contacting us directly is usually faster than a bank dispute.
