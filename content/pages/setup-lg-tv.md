## IPTV on Lg TV: Check your webOS version first

Press the **Settings** button, then go to **All Settings**, then **General** (or **Support**), then **About This TV**. Note the webOS version:

- **webOS 6 and newer (2021+):** the widest choice of players and the newest versions.
- **webOS 4 to 5 (2018–2020):** most major players still install.
- **webOS 3 and older:** expect a limited selection. A Fire TV or Google TV stick is usually the better experience.

## Finding a player in the LG Content Store

1. Press **Home** on the remote and open **Apps** (the LG Content Store).
2. Use **Search** and type *IPTV* or the name of a specific player.
3. Install the one you want. Popular choices on LG include IBO Player, Smart IPTV, SS IPTV and Duplex Play. Availability changes over time.

The setup style splits into two groups.

### IPTV on Lg TV: Players that link through a website

IBO Player, Smart IPTV and Duplex Play show a **MAC address** (and sometimes a **device key**) when you first open them. You then go to the developer's official website on your phone, enter those codes, and add your M3U link or Xtream login there. Restart the app and your list appears. Our [IBO Player guide](/apps/ibo-player) walks through each step, including what to do if the playlist doesn't show up.

### Players that log in on the TV

Some players let you type the Xtream server, username and password with the on-screen keyboard. The **Magic Remote's pointer** makes this much easier than arrow keys. Just aim and click each letter.

## Make live TV look right on LG

LG's default picture modes are tuned for showroom floors. A few changes help streamed TV a lot:

- **Picture mode:** choose *Standard* or *Filmmaker Mode* for movies, and *Sports* only if you like a brighter, punchier look for games.
- **TruMotion:** set it to *Off* or *Smooth* with low de-judder. High settings cause the "soap opera effect" and can create artifacts on compressed live streams.
- **Noise reduction:** turn *Noise Reduction* and *MPEG Noise Reduction* to *Low* for HD channels. That softens compression blockiness without smearing detail.
- **Quick Start+:** turning it off uses a bit more time at power-on, but a full restart clears app memory, which helps if the player gets sluggish after days of use.

## Network tips specific to LG

Many LG TVs show a weak Wi-Fi signal because the antenna sits behind the panel near the wall. If you see buffering:

- Check signal strength under **Settings**, then **Network**, then **Wi-Fi Connection**, then **Advanced Wi-Fi Settings**.
- Connect to your router's **5 GHz** band rather than 2.4 GHz.
- Use Ethernet if a cable can reach. Even a basic wired connection is steadier than marginal Wi-Fi.

If the guide grid shows "No information," the player isn't receiving guide data. See [how the IPTV TV guide works](/guides/iptv-epg) for the fix.


When choosing a reliable **IPTV on Lg TV**, verifying network peering and server routing ensures optimal playback stability.
