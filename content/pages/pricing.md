## IPTV Subscription Service: How to choose a plan
### Essential recommendations for IPTV Subscription Service


**Plan length.** Plans run for 3, 6 or 12 months, and the monthly cost drops as the term gets longer. If you're new to IPTV, the 3-month plan is the smallest commitment and gives you time to confirm everything works on your devices and home network.

**Number of accounts.** Each length is available with 1 to 4 accounts. Adding accounts costs less than buying separate plans: on the 12-month term, one account is $79.99 and two accounts are $119.99.

## Before you order

Run through these checks first, because they prevent most first-week problems:

- **Check your device.** Our [setup guides](/setup) cover Fire TV, Android TV, Samsung, LG, Apple and more, and they show which player apps run on each one.
- **Check your connection.** Plan on about 10 Mbps for each HD stream and 25 Mbps for 4K. A speed test on the TV itself tells you more than one on your phone.
- **Pick a player.** Compare options on our [apps page](/apps). Some player apps charge their own fee, which is separate from these prices.

## What each plan costs per month

The tables above show each total and what it works out to per month. With one account, the monthly cost falls from $13.33 on the 3-month plan to $9.50 on the 6-month plan and $6.67 on the 12-month plan. With four accounts, it falls from $33.33 to $22.50 to $15.00.

A longer plan lowers the monthly cost, but you pay more up front. If you haven't tested IPTV on your own TV and home network yet, a shorter plan keeps your first payment smaller.

## What isn't included

Your plan doesn't include a streaming device or internet service. If you choose a paid player app such as TiviMate Premium or IBO Player, its fee goes to the app's developer. Our [apps page](/apps) explains which players are free and which charge a fee.
