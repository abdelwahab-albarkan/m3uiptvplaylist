## IPTV Questions: Getting started

### IPTV Questions: Which devices can I use?

Fire TV Stick and Fire TV Cube, Android TV and Google TV devices, Samsung and LG smart TVs, Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, Windows and Mac computers, and MAG or Formuler boxes. Roku is the most limited. See [IPTV on Roku](/setup/roku) for workarounds, and the [setup hub](/setup) for everything else.

### What's the easiest device for a beginner?

A Fire TV Stick 4K Plus or 4K Max, or a Google TV device. They run the most player apps, and our [Fire TV guide](/setup/firestick) takes about 10 minutes from start to finish.

### Do I need to buy a player app?

Many players are free, such as IPTV Smarters Pro, XCIPTV, VLC and Kodi. Some charge for premium features or activation, such as TiviMate Premium and IBO Player. Those payments go to the app developer, not to us. Compare them on our [apps page](/apps).

## Your account

### What's the difference between devices and connections?

You can install the app on as many devices as you like. *Connections* is how many of them can stream **at the same moment**.

### Can I use my subscription when I travel?

The service works over the internet, so it can be used away from home on a good connection, such as a hotel or a vacation rental. Hotel Wi-Fi is often slow or blocks streaming ports, so test before you rely on it.

### I forgot my login details. What now?

Check the welcome email from your order first. If you can't find it, [contact support](/contact) from the email address you used to order.

## Picture and performance

### How fast does my internet need to be?

About 10 Mbps per HD stream and 25 Mbps per 4K stream that plays at the same time. Stability matters more than peak speed. See the [buffering guide](/troubleshooting/iptv-buffering).

### Why is my stream behind live TV?

All streaming runs a little behind broadcast because of encoding, delivery and buffering. A delay of 20 to 60 seconds is normal. Our [sports guide](/guides/sports-on-iptv) explains how buffer size affects it.

### Why is the TV guide empty?

The guide (EPG) data hasn't loaded or doesn't match your channels. Refresh it in your player's settings, then follow the [EPG guide](/guides/iptv-epg).

### Can I watch in 4K?

You'll need a 4K TV, a 4K-capable device and a steady connection of about 25 Mbps per stream. Our [4K guide](/guides/4k-8k-iptv) shows how to check whether a stream is true 4K.

## Other questions

### Is IPTV legal?

The technology is legal. Whether a service is legal depends on whether it holds the rights to what it streams. Read [the legal side of IPTV in the US](/guides/is-iptv-legal-usa).

### Do I need a VPN?

Most people don't. A VPN helps in specific cases, such as internet provider throttling. Our [VPN guide](/troubleshooting/vpn-for-iptv) shows how to test whether you need one.

### How do I get help?

Use the [troubleshooting triage](/troubleshooting) for a quick fix, or [contact us](/contact) with your device, app and the exact error message.
