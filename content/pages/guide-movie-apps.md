## Best Movie Apps for Firestick: Free apps with ads

### Essential recommendations for Best Movie Apps for Firestick

These apps are legal and funded by advertising. No subscription is needed, although some offer extra features if you make an account.

- **Tubi:** a large on-demand library of movies and TV shows, with a good mix of older favorites and lesser-known films.
- **Pluto TV:** live-style channels plus on-demand titles. It's a good choice if you don't want to spend time deciding what to watch.
- **The Roku Channel:** despite the name, it's also available on other platforms. It offers free movies, shows and live channels.
- **Plex:** it's best known for personal media libraries, but it also offers free ad-supported movies and channels.
- **Amazon's free content:** Prime Video's interface includes some free, ad-supported titles, without the need for a Prime membership. Look for the "Free with ads" label.

Catalogs change every month, so if you want a particular film, search for it across apps instead of assuming where it is.

## Free with a library card

These are among the most overlooked apps in streaming:

- **Kanopy:** classic films, documentaries and independent movies, without ads. Your library sets how many titles you can watch.
- **Hoopla:** movies, TV, audiobooks and more, also through participating libraries, with borrowing limits.

Check your local library's website to see which of them it supports. They're a great option for parents, film fans and anyone who wants ad-free viewing at no cost.

## Paid subscriptions

If you want newer releases or exclusive originals, compare these services by what you actually watch:

| Service | Known for |
| --- | --- |
| Netflix | Large catalog and originals |
| Prime Video | Included with Prime; rentals and purchases too |
| Max | HBO series and Warner films |
| Disney+ | Disney, Pixar, Marvel and Star Wars |
| Hulu | Next-day TV and originals |
| Paramount+ | Paramount films and CBS shows |
| Peacock | Universal films and NBC shows |

Names, plans and catalogs change, so check each service's current offer. To save money, subscribe to one or two at a time and rotate them. Many have monthly plans that you can cancel between shows.

## Renting without subscribing

If you only want to watch one new movie, renting can be cheaper than a subscription. You can rent or buy many films through Prime Video and other stores. Compare the price between stores, because they can differ on the same title.

## Which app suits you?

- **Lots of free movies:** Tubi
- **Channel surfing:** Pluto TV
- **Ad-free at no cost:** Kanopy or Hoopla, through your library
- **Kids:** Disney+, or the kids' sections and profiles in free apps
- **The newest movies:** rentals, or the paid service that has them

## Apps to avoid

Be skeptical of sites that list "best Firestick movie apps" and tell you to sideload an app offering every new film for free. Those apps typically have no rights to the content. They can stop working overnight, and some carry malware or intrusive ads. If an article gives you a [Downloader code](/guides/firestick-downloader-codes) to install one, check it carefully before going further.

Stay safe with these simple rules:

1. Install from the Amazon Appstore whenever possible.
2. Check that the publisher matches the service you expect.
3. Never pay for "lifetime" access to a free or unknown app.
4. Remove any app that asks for unrelated permissions or your card details.
