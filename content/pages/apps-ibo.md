## How IBO Player works

Smart TV remotes make typing long playlist links painful, so IBO Player uses a two-device setup:

1. The TV app shows a **MAC address** and a **device key**.
2. On your phone or computer, you enter those codes on the developer's website and add your playlist there.
3. The TV downloads the playlist the next time the app starts.

## Step-by-step setup

### IBO Player: 1. Install the app

On Samsung, open **Apps** and search *IBO Player*. On LG, open the **LG Content Store**. On Android TV or Google TV, use the Play Store. Install and open it.

### 2. Note your codes

The first screen shows the **Mac Address** (six pairs of letters and numbers, like `A1:B2:C3:D4:E5:F6`) and the **Device Key**. Take a photo with your phone so you don't mistype them.

### 3. Upload your playlist on the official site

- Open the developer's official website. The address is shown inside the app, so type it from there rather than searching, because look-alike sites exist.
- Go to the **Manage playlist** or **Login** section and enter the MAC address and device key.
- Choose **Add playlist**. Enter a name and either your **M3U link**, or your **Xtream server, username and password** if Xtream is offered.
- Optionally set a **PIN** to protect the playlist from being edited.

### 4. Restart the app on your TV

Fully close the app (on Samsung, hold the Back button) and reopen it. Select your playlist. Channels, movies and series load in a minute or two.

## Activation after the trial

When the trial ends, the app shows a payment notice. Pay only on the developer's official site, the same one you used to upload the playlist. If anyone else offers to "activate IBO for free," treat it as a scam. Our [IPTV scams guide](/guides/iptv-scams) covers similar tricks.

## Useful settings

- **Change language and time format** under Settings so guide times show in 12-hour US format.
- **Change the player engine** if a channel won't play. The settings menu offers alternatives that handle some stream formats better.
- **Parental lock** hides selected categories behind the PIN.

## Troubleshooting IBO Player

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "No playlist" after uploading | Double-check the MAC address; restart the app fully; confirm you saved the playlist on the site |
| Playlist loads but channels fail | Test the same login on another device; if it fails there too, contact your provider |
| Guide is empty | With M3U, make sure an EPG link is included; see our [EPG guide](/guides/iptv-epg) |
| App asks to activate again | Your trial or yearly activation expired; renew on the official site only |
