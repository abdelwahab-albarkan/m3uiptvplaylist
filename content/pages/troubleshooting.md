## IPTV Troubleshooting: The two-minute triage
### Essential recommendations for IPTV Troubleshooting


Work through these questions in order. Each answer points you to a specific guide.

1. **Does anything load at all?** If the app shows an error such as *authorization failed* or *server not responding*, or an empty channel list, it's a login or connection problem. Go to [IPTV not working](/troubleshooting/iptv-not-working).
2. **Does it play, but pause or stutter?** That's buffering. Go to the [buffering guide](/troubleshooting/iptv-buffering).
3. **Is it only one channel?** A single channel that fails while others play is almost always a problem with that stream, not your setup. Try again later or report it to support with the channel name.
4. **Is it only one device?** If the phone plays but the TV doesn't, the TV's Wi-Fi signal or app settings are the cause. Compare a speed test on both.
5. **Is it only at certain times?** Problems every evening from about 7 to 11 p.m. point to network congestion, either at home or at your internet provider. The [VPN and throttling guide](/troubleshooting/vpn-for-iptv) explains how to tell.

## Do these three things first

They fix a surprising share of issues, whatever the symptom:

- **Restart the device properly.** Unplug the TV or stick from power for 30 seconds. Pressing the remote's power button usually just puts it to sleep.
- **Restart the router.** Same idea: unplug for 30 seconds, then wait two minutes for it to reconnect.
- **Update the player app.** Old versions break when servers or streaming formats change.

## Common causes by symptom

| What you notice | Most likely cause | Where to start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pauses every few seconds | Slow or unstable connection | [Buffering guide](/troubleshooting/iptv-buffering) |
| Choppy motion with no loading icon | Frame-rate mismatch | Turn on frame-rate matching in your device settings |
| Login error | A typo in the details, or an expired plan | [Error guide](/troubleshooting/iptv-not-working) |
| Sound with a black picture | Video decoder problem | Switch between hardware and software decoding |
| Empty guide | Guide data missing or mismatched | [EPG guide](/guides/iptv-epg) |
| Slower only through a VPN | Distant or overloaded VPN server | [Firestick VPN guide](/troubleshooting/vpn-for-firestick) |

## Keep a short log for recurring problems

If a problem comes and goes, restarting won't reveal the pattern. For two or three evenings, note the time, the channel, the device and what you were doing on the network. A problem at the same time each night points to congestion. If it only happens on one channel, the stream itself is the likely cause. If it follows one TV from room to room, look at that device first.

## What to tell support

If you need help, a good report gets a fast answer. Include your **device** (for example *Fire TV Stick 4K Max*), the **player app and version**, the **exact error message**, **which channels** are affected, and **when** it started. A speed test result from the TV itself helps too.
