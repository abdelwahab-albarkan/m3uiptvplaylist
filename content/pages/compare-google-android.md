## Google TV vs Android TV: The simple explanation

### Essential recommendations for Google TV vs Android TV

Think of Android TV as the engine and Google TV as the dashboard. Android TV handles apps, updates and hardware support. Google TV adds a home screen focused on recommendations, your watchlist and content from across your services.

Android TV itself also has its own, simpler home screen. So when people compare the two, they're usually comparing **interfaces**, not two different platforms.

## Which should you choose?

You usually don't choose the interface separately, because it comes with the device. Base your decision on the hardware instead:

- **Speed:** More memory and a faster processor matter more than which home screen you get.
- **Updates:** A recent device from a major brand is more likely to keep getting updates.
- **Ports:** Check for Ethernet if your Wi-Fi is unreliable. The Google TV Streamer has an Ethernet port, for example.
- **Certification:** Buy a device with Google certification. Uncertified boxes sold as "Android TV" may run a modified version of Android and may carry security risks. Read our [IPTV box guide](/guides/iptv-box) for details.

## What about the end of Chromecast?

Google stopped making its Chromecast devices and introduced the Google TV Streamer as the replacement. If you already own a Chromecast with Google TV, you don't need to rush out and replace it while it's still running well and getting support.

The Streamer adds more memory and storage, along with an Ethernet port. If you're buying new, compare it with other Google TV devices, such as Walmart's onn. 4K boxes, or a TV with Google TV already built in.

## Using IPTV apps on either one

The steps are the same on both:

1. Install a player from Google Play, or sideload it from the developer's website if needed.
2. Add your login.
3. Turn on frame-rate matching for smoother sports and movies.

Our [Android TV and Google TV guide](/setup/android-tv) covers each step, including the settings paths that differ between the two interfaces. Most popular players, such as [TiviMate](/apps/tivimate), work on both.

## Clean up the Google TV home screen

If you find Google TV's recommendations too busy, try these options:

- **Turn on apps-only mode** in your account or profile settings, if your device offers it. This removes personalized recommendations but also limits some Google features.
- **Rearrange your favorite apps** so that the ones you use most sit first.
- **Add services you subscribe to** so that the recommendations are more useful, and remove any you don't use.
- **Check privacy controls** in your Google account to manage personalized results.

## If you're buying a TV

Many Sony, TCL and Hisense models use Google TV, though brands also sell the same size TV with other systems. Don't choose a TV for its interface alone, because a streaming stick can replace the built-in software if you later change your mind. Picture quality is the part you can't change, so prioritize it.

## The bottom line

Don't worry much about the label. Both run the same apps from the same store. Pick a certified device with enough memory and a recent update history, and use the home screen you're given.
