## TiviMate IPTV Player: Why people choose TiviMate

### Essential recommendations for TiviMate IPTV Player

- **A real grid guide** with channel logos, program titles and progress bars, and it handles thousands of channels without lag.
- **Fast channel switching** with the remote's up and down buttons, plus a last-channel button.
- **Catch-up and recording** on Premium, when your provider supports catch-up.
- **Favorites and custom groups** so you can hide hundreds of channels you'll never watch.

The trade-off is that it runs only on Android-based TV devices, and the best features need Premium.

## Install TiviMate

- **Google TV / Android TV:** search *TiviMate* in the Play Store and install.
- **Fire TV:** search the Amazon Appstore first. If it isn't listed for your device, follow the Downloader method in our [Fire TV guide](/setup/firestick) and use the developer's official download address.

## Add your playlist

1. Open TiviMate and choose **Add playlist**.
2. Select **Xtream Codes** (recommended) or **M3U playlist**.
3. For Xtream: enter the **server address**, **username** and **password**. For M3U: enter the URL, or choose *Enter URL on another device* to type it from your phone.
4. Name the playlist, choose whether to include VOD (movies and series), and finish.
5. TiviMate downloads the channel list. With Xtream Codes, the TV guide usually loads automatically. With M3U, add your EPG link under **Settings**, then **EPG**, then **Add EPG source**.

## Activate Premium with TiviMate Companion

1. Install **TiviMate Companion** on an Android phone from Google Play.
2. Create an account in Companion and buy Premium (yearly or one-time).
3. On the TV, go to **Settings**, then **Account**, then **Sign in**, and enter the same account details.

Buy only through the Companion app. "Lifetime TiviMate codes" sold elsewhere are often reused or revoked.

## Settings that make a difference

- **Playback, then AFR (auto frame rate): On.** Matches the TV's refresh rate to each channel, so you get smoother sports and no judder on 24 fps movies.
- **Playback, then Buffer size: Medium or Large.** Larger buffers start a second slower but ride out short network dips. See the [buffering guide](/troubleshooting/iptv-buffering).
- **EPG, then Update on app start: On**, with a daily update interval, so the guide is never empty in the morning.
- **EPG, then time shift:** if program times are off by exactly one or several hours, adjust the time shift for that guide source.
- **Interface, then Hide channel groups** you don't use. A shorter list makes every remote press faster.
- **Parental control:** set a PIN and lock adult or late-night groups if kids use the TV.

## Common TiviMate problems

- **"No information" in the guide:** the EPG source isn't loaded, or channel IDs don't match. Our [EPG guide](/guides/iptv-epg) explains both fixes.
- **Playlist won't update:** go to **Settings**, then **Playlists**, select yours, and choose **Update playlist**. Then restart the app.
- **Black screen with sound:** in **Settings**, then **Playback**, switch the video decoder between hardware and software for that channel.
