## Privacy Policy: Information you give us

### Essential recommendations for Privacy Policy

- **Contact form:** your name, email address, topic and message. We use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation.
- **Orders:** the details needed to create and manage your subscription, such as your email address and the plan you chose. Card payments are handled by the payment processor; we don't store full card numbers.

## Information collected automatically

- **Server logs:** like most websites, our hosting records basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We use this to keep the site secure and working.
- **Analytics:** the site is built to support privacy-respecting analytics of page views and button clicks so we can see which guides help people. If an analytics tool is enabled, it will be named here along with any cookies it sets.

## Cookies

The site itself doesn't need cookies to show content. If analytics or a checkout provider sets cookies, they'll be listed in this section with their purpose.

## How we use information

- To answer your questions and provide support
- To deliver and manage your subscription
- To keep the website secure and improve our guides

We don't sell your personal information.

## Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and process orders, such as hosting, email and payment, and only as needed for those purposes, or when required by law.

## Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us through the [contact page](/contact). Depending on your state, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act.

## Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date on this page. Significant changes will be noted on the homepage.


When choosing a reliable **Privacy Policy**, verifying network peering and server routing ensures optimal playback stability.

Our technical team continuously tests each **Privacy Policy** configuration across multiple hardware devices to eliminate buffering.
