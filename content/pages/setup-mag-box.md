## Mag IPTV Box: Before you start

### Essential recommendations for Mag IPTV Box

You need three things:

- **The MAC address** from the sticker on the bottom of the box
- **A portal URL** from your provider, created for that MAC address
- **A network connection,** ideally an Ethernet cable from the router

Send your provider the MAC address first. The portal won't load until it's registered, and a mistyped character is one of the most common reasons a new box fails.

## Step 1: Connect the box

1. Connect HDMI from the box to your TV.
2. Plug in the Ethernet cable. Wi-Fi may need an adapter, depending on the model.
3. Connect the power cable and switch on the TV's matching HDMI input.

## Step 2: Open the system settings

As the box starts, the inner portal or loading screen appears. Press **Settings** or **Setup** on the remote, or follow the on-screen prompt, to open **System settings**. Menu names can differ slightly by model and firmware.

## Step 3: Add the portal

1. Choose **Servers**, then **Portals**.
2. In **Portal 1 name**, enter any label, such as *Home TV*.
3. In **Portal 1 URL**, enter the full address from your provider. Include `http://` and the port and path, exactly as written.
4. Press **OK** to save.

The remote's small keyboard makes URLs easy to mistype. Check slashes and dots carefully, especially the difference between `0` and `O`.

## Step 4: Restart and test

Go back to the main settings page and choose **Restart portal**, or reboot the box. If everything is correct, the provider's interface loads with its channel list. The first load can take a little longer than later ones.

## Good settings to check

- **Video:** set the output to match your TV, such as 1080p or a 4K mode on supported models. Turn on automatic frame rate if your firmware has it.
- **Time zone:** set your US time zone under **Time** settings, or the TV guide can appear shifted. See our [EPG guide](/guides/iptv-epg).
- **Firmware:** use the box's update option for official updates. Avoid custom firmware files from forums.

## Fixing common MAG problems

| What you see | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Your STB is blocked" | MAC isn't active on the portal | Confirm the address and account status with your provider |
| Stuck on "Loading portal" | Wrong URL or no network | Re-enter the URL and test the network connection |
| Black screen on some channels | Stream or codec issue | Try other channels; older boxes may not decode HEVC |
| "Authentication failed" | Registration not finished, or a date and time issue | Restart, check the time setting, then contact your provider |
| Regular stuttering | Weak connection | Use Ethernet, then follow the [buffering guide](/troubleshooting/iptv-buffering) |

## Is a MAG box right for you?

MAG boxes suit people who want one simple, cable-like interface that rarely changes, and whose provider supports portals. They're less flexible than Android devices because you can't easily change apps. Before buying, ask whether your provider supports MAG portals, and check whether the model can decode H.265 if you want 4K.
