## IPTV on Firestick: What you'll need
### Essential recommendations for IPTV on Firestick


If your TV is older, read [how to use a stick on a non-smart TV](/guides/fire-stick-non-smart-tv) first.


- A Fire TV Stick running Fire OS (such as the 4K Plus or 4K Max, not the 4K Select or 2026 HD), a Fire TV Cube, or a TV with Fire TV built in, such as Insignia, Toshiba or Amazon Omni models
- Your IPTV login: either an Xtream Codes server, username and password, or an M3U link
- About 10 minutes and a stable connection. The 4K Max has an Ethernet option through Amazon's adapter, which helps a lot on busy Wi-Fi.

> **Check your model first.** Most existing Fire TV devices run Fire OS, which is based on Android, and can install outside apps. The Fire TV Stick 4K Select, the 2026 Fire TV Stick HD and future Fire TV Sticks run Amazon's Vega OS and can't. If the steps below don't match your menus, look up your exact model under Settings, then My Fire TV, then About.

## Step 1: Install Downloader

Downloader is a free utility from AFTVnews that's listed in the Amazon Appstore. It's the standard, safe way to fetch an app file onto a Fire TV.

1. From the home screen, open **Find**, then **Search**, and type *Downloader*.
2. Select the orange Downloader app and choose **Get** or **Download**.
3. Open it once, and allow access to files if asked.

## Step 2: Turn on Developer options

1. Go to **Settings**, then **My Fire TV**, then **About**.
2. Highlight your device name, for example *Fire TV Stick 4K*, and press select **seven times**.
3. Press back. **Developer options** now appears under My Fire TV.

## Step 3: Allow Downloader to install apps

1. Open **Settings**, then **My Fire TV**, then **Developer options**.
2. Choose **Install unknown apps**. On older models this is a single switch called *Apps from Unknown Sources*.
3. Switch **Downloader** to **ON**. Leave every other app off. You only need to trust the one tool you just installed.

## Step 4: Download your player

Open Downloader and type the official download address of the player you've chosen, or the short code its developer publishes. Short codes are just bookmarks that point to a web address. Only use codes from the app developer's own website, never from random lists. Our [Downloader codes guide](/guides/firestick-downloader-codes) shows how to check one first.

When the download finishes, choose **Install**, then **Done**, and delete the file when Downloader offers. That frees up storage, which matters on the 8 GB models.

If you'd rather skip sideloading entirely, search the Amazon Appstore first. Several IPTV players are listed there directly.

## Step 5: Add your login

Open the player and pick the login type that matches what you received:

- **Xtream Codes API:** enter the server URL exactly as given, including `http://` and any port number such as `:8080`. Then enter your username and password.
- **M3U playlist:** paste the full link. Typing a long URL with the remote is painful, so many players let you enter it from your phone through a QR code or a web page.

The first load can take a minute while the channel list and guide download. After that, startup is quick.

## Getting the best picture on Fire TV

- **Match frame rate.** Go to **Settings**, then **Display & Sounds**, then **Display**, and turn on **Match Original Frame Rate**. Live sports at 60 fps and movies at 24 fps then play without judder.
- **Clear space.** Fire TV slows down when storage is nearly full. Uninstall apps you don't use under **Settings**, then **Applications**, then **Manage Installed Applications**.
- **Restart weekly.** Hold **Select** and **Play/Pause** together for about five seconds to restart. It clears memory leaks that build up over days.
- **Keep it cool.** Sticks tucked behind a wall-mounted TV can overheat and throttle. The included HDMI extender moves the stick into open air.

## If something goes wrong

If the player says *authorization failed*, re-check the username and password. They're case-sensitive, and autocorrect on the on-screen keyboard can add spaces. If channels load but stutter, work through the [buffering guide](/troubleshooting/iptv-buffering). If nothing loads at all, see [IPTV not working](/troubleshooting/iptv-not-working).
