## Free Live TV Streaming: What "free live TV" really means
### Essential recommendations for Free Live TV Streaming


Free ad-supported streaming TV, often shortened to FAST, works like old-fashioned broadcast TV. Channels run on a schedule, ad breaks pay for them, and you don't pay a subscription. Many channels focus on one thing, such as classic game shows, crime dramas, news or a single long-running series.

What you generally won't find are the big paid cable networks and most major live sports. If those matter to you, compare paid options in our guide to [live TV streaming services](/compare/live-tv-streaming-services).

## The main free services

| Service | Good for | Where it runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pluto TV | A traditional channel guide with a wide mix of genres | Most TVs, streaming devices, phones and browsers |
| Tubi | A large on-demand library, plus live channels | Most TVs, streaming devices, phones and browsers |
| The Roku Channel | Live channels and on-demand content, especially on Roku | Roku devices, plus other platforms and the web |
| Samsung TV Plus | Built-in channels with no separate install | Samsung TVs and Galaxy devices, plus the web |
| LG Channels | Built-in channels on LG sets | LG TVs and other LG devices |
| Xumo Play | Live channels and free movies | Many TVs, streaming devices and browsers |

Channel lineups change without much notice, so if you're after a specific show, search for it inside the app before you set anything else up.

## Add an antenna for local channels

An antenna fills the biggest gap in free streaming. Local ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX and PBS stations broadcast over the air, and those networks carry plenty of national news, prime-time shows and major games. Check the FCC's DTV Reception Maps to see which stations reach your address, then read our [local channels guide](/guides/iptv-local-channels) for setup tips.

## How to build a free lineup in 15 minutes

1. **Start with what you own.** Samsung and LG TVs already have their free channels built in, and Roku devices have The Roku Channel.
2. **Add one or two apps.** Pluto TV for channel surfing and Tubi for on-demand movies is a good starting pair.
3. **Save your favorites.** Most apps let you mark channels so you don't have to scroll past hundreds of them.
4. **Connect an antenna** if you get good reception, then run the TV's channel scan.
5. **Hide what you don't use.** Move your free apps to the front of your home screen so they're as easy to reach as cable was.

## How to spot unsafe "free TV" sites

Searches for free TV also turn up plenty of sites you should avoid. Be careful if a site or app:

- Offers every premium channel, pay-per-view fight or new movie at no cost
- Asks you to install a browser extension, "video codec" or unknown file before playing
- Fills the screen with pop-ups or fake virus warnings
- Asks for your card number to "verify your age" on a free service
- Only exists as an APK you have to download from a random site

These sites usually don't have rights to the content, and the risk to your device and your payment details is real. Our [IPTV scams guide](/guides/iptv-scams) covers more warning signs.

## When free isn't enough

Free services work well as background TV and as a way to lower your bills, but you may want to pay if you need specific sports, a full cable-style lineup or a guaranteed set of local stations.
