## Cheapest Live TV Streaming: Start with a list of must-haves

### Essential recommendations for Cheapest Live TV Streaming

Before you compare prices, write down what your household won't give up. Most decisions come down to four questions:

1. **Do you need local channels?** If so, can an antenna handle them at your address?
2. **Which sports do you follow,** and which networks carry them? National games, regional teams and league packages are often sold differently.
3. **How many people watch at the same time?** Each service has its own limit on simultaneous streams.
4. **Do you record shows?** Cloud DVR limits and storage rules differ from one service to another.

Your answers usually eliminate half the options before price even comes up.

Lineups change, and carriage disputes can remove channels. Treat this table as a starting point, then enter your ZIP code on each service's site.

## Work out the real monthly cost

Compare the full cost rather than the advertised price:

- **Taxes and regional fees.** Some services add charges for local broadcast or regional sports channels.
- **Add-ons.** A sports pack or extra DVR space can erase the gap between a cheap plan and a broader one.
- **Promotions.** Check what you'll pay once an introductory rate ends.
- **Stream limits.** If your household needs more screens than the plan allows, price the upgrade.
- **Double payments.** A bundle can save money, or it can duplicate something you already pay for elsewhere.

A useful test is to price your exact must-have list on each service, then divide by how many months a year you'd keep it. A service you cancel in the off-season can beat a cheaper one you keep all year.

## Three ways to spend less

**Pair a smaller plan with an antenna.** Local broadcasts carry a lot of major sports and news. An antenna can cover those channels, which may let you choose a smaller plan. Our [local channels guide](/guides/iptv-local-channels) explains how to check reception at your address.

**Rotate services by season.** Since most plans run month to month, you can keep a sports-heavy plan during the season and drop it afterward. Set a calendar reminder before each renewal date.

**Use free services for background TV.** Pluto TV, Tubi, The Roku Channel and Samsung TV Plus cover news, older shows and movies at no cost, which can reduce how much you need from a paid plan.

## Test before you commit

Many services offer a free trial or promotional first period, although terms change. Use that time to test what matters on your own equipment:

- Watch at a busy hour, such as a weekend evening or during a big game.
- Find your local stations and regional teams in the guide.
- Record something, then play it back and try skipping forward.
- Stream on two screens at once if that's how your family watches.

If playback stutters, the service may not be the problem. Our [buffering guide](/troubleshooting/iptv-buffering) can help you find out whether your home network is the cause.
