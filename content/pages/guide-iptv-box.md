## The four main options
### Essential recommendations for IPTV Box


| Type | Examples | Strengths | Trade-offs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Streaming stick / streamer | Fire TV Stick 4K Plus or 4K Max, Google TV Streamer, Chromecast | Cheap, supported, runs every popular player | Limited storage; Wi-Fi only on some models |
| Premium Android TV box | Nvidia Shield TV Pro | Fast, Gigabit Ethernet, great upscaling | Expensive |
| IPTV-focused Android box | Formuler, Buzz TV | Remote with number keys, IPTV-tuned software | Smaller companies; check update history |
| Linux portal box | MAG (by Infomir) | Simple, stable, cable-box feel | Needs a provider that supports portals; fewer apps |

## Streaming sticks: the default choice

For most people, a **Fire TV Stick 4K Plus or 4K Max** or a **Google TV device** is the best IPTV box, even though it isn't sold as one. They cost little, get security updates, and run TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro and every other major player. Skip the Fire TV Stick 4K Select for this job, since it runs Vega OS and can't sideload. Our [Fire TV](/setup/firestick) and [Android TV](/setup/android-tv) guides cover setup.

## Formuler and similar IPTV boxes

See our [Formuler setup guide](/setup/formuler) if you already have one.

Formuler boxes run Android with a custom app called **MyTVOnline**, designed for IPTV logins. Their remotes have number keys and dedicated guide buttons, which older family members often prefer. Before buying, check that the model still gets updates and supports the Google Play Store.

## MAG boxes

Our [MAG box setup guide](/setup/mag-box) has the full walkthrough. In short:

MAG boxes from Infomir don't use player apps in the usual way. They connect to a **portal** run by the IPTV provider:

1. The provider registers the box's **MAC address**, printed on the bottom.
2. In **Settings**, then **Servers**, then **Portals**, you enter the portal URL the provider gives you.
3. The box restarts and loads the provider's interface.

MAG boxes are stable and simple, but only work with providers that offer portal access, and you can't easily switch to another player app.

## Why to avoid cheap unbranded Android boxes

Our [Android TV box buying guide](/guides/android-tv-box) has the full checklist. The main problems:

Marketplaces are full of $40 boxes with names like "Android 13 8K Ultra." The problems:

- **Malware.** In June 2025 the FBI warned that the BADBOX 2.0 botnet had infected millions of off-brand streaming devices, often before they were sold.
- **Fake specs.** "8K" and "Android 13" labels are often just stickers on old hardware running outdated software.
- **No updates.** Security holes never get patched.
- **Preloaded apps.** "Fully loaded" boxes usually include apps with unlicensed streams. Read [our scams guide](/guides/iptv-scams) for why that's a risk to you.

If a box isn't **Google TV / Android TV certified** (it shows the Play Store and Google's certification in settings) and isn't from a known brand, skip it.

## What to look for in any box

- **Ethernet port,** ideally Gigabit
- **HEVC (H.265) hardware decoding** for 4K streams, plus AV1 for future-proofing
- **At least 2 GB of RAM** and 16 GB of storage for smooth guide scrolling
- **Recent software updates** from the manufacturer
