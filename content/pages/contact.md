## IPTV Customer Support: Get a faster answer
### Essential recommendations for IPTV Customer Support


Support questions are much quicker to solve when we know:

- **Your device,** for example *Fire TV Stick 4K Max* or *Samsung 2022 QLED*
- **Your player app** and its version, for example *TiviMate 5.x*
- **The exact error message,** or a short description of what happens
- **Which channels** are affected and **when** it started

If you have a minute, try our [troubleshooting triage](/troubleshooting) first. It often finds the fix in a couple of minutes.

## Never send these in a message

We will never ask for your Amazon, Google, Apple or bank passwords, or for remote access to your phone. If someone claiming to be IPTV M3U Playlist support asks for any of these, it isn't us. You also don't need to send your IPTV password in the contact form. Your device, app and the error message are enough for us to start.

## Common questions we can help with

- Choosing the right plan and number of connections. See [pricing](/pricing).
- Installing a player on your device. Our [setup guides](/setup) cover every major platform.
- Fixing buffering, login errors and an empty guide
- Reseller program inquiries

## Before you write

Many questions are already answered in the [FAQ](/faq), including how connections work, what internet speed you need, and why streams run a little behind live TV. If you're stuck on a specific device, the matching setup guide usually has a troubleshooting section at the end.
