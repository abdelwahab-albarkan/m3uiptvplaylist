## Best IPTV App for Apple TV: Choosing a player

### Essential recommendations for Best IPTV App for Apple TV

The App Store has dozens of IPTV players. They don't include channels; you bring your own login. When you compare them, check four things in the listing:

1. **Supports Xtream Codes**, not just M3U. Xtream loads movies, series and the guide more cleanly.
2. **Has a grid EPG** (TV guide), ideally with a time-shift setting.
3. **Is available on tvOS**, if you want to use it on Apple TV and not just your phone.
4. **Was updated recently.** Players that haven't been updated in a year often break with new iOS versions.

Commonly used options include IPTV Smarters Pro (free, simple), iPlayTV (Apple TV focused), GSE Smart IPTV (feature-heavy), and newer players like IPTVX and UHF with a more modern, Apple-style design. Read our [player comparison](/apps) for what each does best.

## Setting up on Apple TV

1. Open the **App Store** on Apple TV and search for your chosen player.
2. Install it and open it. Choose **Add playlist**, then **Xtream Codes** or **M3U URL**.
3. Enter your details. Many tvOS players show a **QR code** or a short web address so you can type the login on your iPhone. Use it, because the Siri Remote keyboard is slow.
4. Wait for the channel list and guide to download, then add favorites.

**Recommended Apple TV settings:** go to **Settings**, then **Video and Audio**, then **Match Content**, and turn on both **Match Dynamic Range** and **Match Frame Rate**. For the most stable stream, use the Ethernet port on the Apple TV 4K (Wi-Fi + Ethernet model).

## Setting up on iPhone and iPad

1. Install the same player from the App Store. If it's a universal purchase, you won't pay twice.
2. Sign in to the same iCloud account if the app syncs, and your playlist appears automatically.
3. Otherwise, add your login the same way as on Apple TV.

On iPad, look for picture-in-picture support. It lets you keep a game in a corner while you use other apps.

## Watching from iPhone on another TV

- **To an Apple TV or AirPlay TV:** tap the AirPlay icon in the player. Most Samsung, LG, Sony and Vizio TVs from 2019 on, plus many Roku models, accept AirPlay.
- **To a Fire TV or Google TV:** AirPlay isn't built in, so it's simpler to install a player directly on that device using our [Fire TV guide](/setup/firestick).

## Troubleshooting on Apple devices

- **"Network connection lost"** on iPhone often means the app lost local network permission. Check **Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**, then **Local Network**.
- **No guide data:** some players need you to refresh the EPG manually after the first login. Look for *Update EPG* in the settings.
- **Playback stops when the screen locks:** enable *background audio* or *picture-in-picture* in the player, if it offers it.


When choosing a reliable **Best IPTV App for Apple TV**, verifying network peering and server routing ensures optimal playback stability.
