## Why free public M3U playlists constantly buffer and die

To understand why free links fail, it helps to analyze how streaming servers deliver video data across the internet:

### Free M3U Playlist vs Premium: 1. Severe server overload and bandwidth exhaustion

When a free **m3u iptv playlist** is published online, thousands of scrapers and viewers connect to the same source IP simultaneously. Standard hosting servers simply do not possess the multi-gigabit network pipes required to deliver packets to such a large audience.
