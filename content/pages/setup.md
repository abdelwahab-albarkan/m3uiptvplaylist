## How to Set Up IPTV: What every setup has in common
### Essential recommendations for How to Set Up IPTV


No matter which device you use, IPTV setup always comes down to the same three steps:

1. **Install a player app.** The player is the software that plays streams and shows the TV guide. It doesn't come with any channels of its own.
2. **Add your login.** Your subscription comes as either an *Xtream Codes* login (a server address, username and password) or an *M3U link* (one long URL). Xtream Codes is easier to type on a TV remote and usually loads movies and series more neatly. Our guide to [M3U playlists and Xtream Codes](/guides/m3u-playlist) explains the difference.
3. **Load the guide.** Most players pull in the TV guide (EPG) automatically with Xtream Codes. With M3U you may need to paste a separate EPG link.

## Before you start

- **Check your connection.** Run a speed test on the device itself, not on your phone. You want at least 10 Mbps for HD and about 25 Mbps for 4K on each screen that will stream.
- **Use Ethernet where you can.** A wired connection fixes more streaming problems than any app setting. If the TV is far from the router, a 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection is the next best option.
- **Have your login ready.** Copy it somewhere you can read it while you type with the remote. Usernames and passwords are case-sensitive.

## How long setup takes

Installing a player from an app store takes a few minutes. Most of your time goes into entering the login, then waiting for the first channel and guide download. Devices that link through a website, such as many Samsung and LG players, take an extra step because you enter a device code on your phone or computer.

Keep your login handy after setup. You'll need the same details for each extra device, and again if you ever reinstall a player or replace your TV.

## Which device should you use?

If you're buying something new, a Fire TV Stick 4K Plus or 4K Max or a Google TV streamer gives you the widest choice of apps, including TiviMate. Avoid the Fire TV Stick 4K Select for IPTV, because it can't install apps from outside the Amazon Appstore. Built-in Samsung and LG apps work well but have fewer player options. Roku is the most limited platform, and Apple TV has excellent apps that often cost a few dollars.

Setting up several TVs? Do the one closest to the router first. If it plays smoothly there, you know the login and app are fine, and any problem on a farther TV is a Wi-Fi coverage issue, not an account issue.
