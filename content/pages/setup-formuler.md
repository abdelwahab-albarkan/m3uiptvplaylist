## Formuler IPTV Box: What you'll need

### Essential recommendations for Formuler IPTV Box

- Your Formuler box, connected to the TV by HDMI
- An internet connection, with Ethernet recommended for stable streams
- Login details from your provider: a **portal URL** tied to the box's MAC address, or an **M3U or Xtream** login if your app version supports it

Your box's MAC address is on a label on the device and in its system information. If your provider uses portal access, send it the address before you begin.

## Step 1: Update and connect

1. Turn on the box and complete the first-time setup.
2. Connect it to your network under **Settings**, then **Network**.
3. Check for a system update. Older software can cause MyTVOnline problems.

Don't install firmware from forums or unofficial sites. Use updates from the manufacturer.

## Step 2: Add your connection in MyTVOnline

1. Open **MyTVOnline** from the home screen.
2. Open the option to **add a connection or portal**. Its exact wording varies by version.
3. Give the connection a name, such as *Home*.
4. Enter the details from your provider:
   - **Portal:** enter the full address, including `http://` and any port.
   - **M3U or Xtream:** enter the link or server, username and password, if offered.
5. Save and let it load. The first channel and guide download can take several minutes.

Enter the details exactly as given. Addresses and passwords are sensitive to small typing errors, and a phone keyboard app or USB keyboard makes entry much easier.

## Step 3: Set up the TV guide

The guide usually comes with your connection. Once it's loaded, open the EPG view to check that listings appear next to your channels. If it's blank, refresh it from the app's settings. If times are wrong, check the box's time zone first. Our [EPG guide](/guides/iptv-epg) goes through other causes.

## Step 4: Organize your channels

A long channel list is the biggest everyday annoyance with any IPTV setup, so spend ten minutes on this:

- **Create favorites** for the channels your family really watches.
- **Hide groups** you'll never use. This makes scrolling faster on any box.
- **Use number keys** to assign familiar positions to favorite channels if your version supports it.
- **Set a parental lock** on any groups you don't want kids to access.

## Settings that improve playback

- **Resolution:** set the output to match your TV, and use automatic frame rate if available so sports and films play at the right rate.
- **Audio:** if you get a picture but no sound, change the audio output from passthrough to PCM.
- **Player choice:** if a channel won't play, try a different playback engine in the settings.
- **Storage:** clear the app's cache occasionally and uninstall apps you don't use.

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Portal won't connect | Check the URL, confirm the MAC address is registered, and restart |
| Channels load but won't play | Test several channels, then try another player engine |
| Empty guide | Refresh the guide and check the time zone |
| Frequent buffering | Use Ethernet and follow our [buffering guide](/troubleshooting/iptv-buffering) |
| App crashes or freezes | Clear its cache, restart the box and check for updates |
